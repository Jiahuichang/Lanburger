## Rules

- A friend NPC is added only after the user approves their character card.
- Only appearance and role go in this file. Private details from the notes stay out of the repo.
- No NPC is based on a public figure or celebrity, because the game is public and they have not consented.
  Characters inspired by one may be **original** designs with original names that don't resemble the person.
- Copyrighted characters (e.g. Pokémon) are never drawn. Use an original stand-in instead.
